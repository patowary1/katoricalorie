Masor Tenga is a tangy and light fish curry traditionally prepared in Assamese households. Unlike heavy cream-based Indian gravies, it uses lemon, tomatoes, or elephant apple which gives it a distinct clean sour profile. Aloo Pitika / আলু পিতিকা Carbohydrate Framework

Aloo Pitika is a comforting mashed potato dish traditionally prepared across Assam. Unlike Western mashed alternatives packed with commercial butter, it features raw mustard oil, raw onions, and chopped green chilies. A standard serving contains around 90 calories. Potatoes render clean complex carbohydrates for glycogen replenishment, and when balanced with minimal raw mustard oil, it offers healthy fat-soluble micro-nutrients without high saturated fat inputs.
