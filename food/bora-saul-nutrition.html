Bora Saul (Sticky Rice) Nutrition

Official KatoriCalorie Recipe & Nutrient Profile

Bora Saul (বৰা চাউল) is an indigenous variety of glutinous sticky rice cultivated widely in Assam. It is characterized by high concentrations of amylopectin starch, which gives cooked grains a sticky, cohesive texture.

While delicious and widely used in traditional Bihu snacks (pithas), Bora Saul has a high glycemic profile and should be portioned carefully. Pair it with high-fiber greens or proteins to slow digestion.
