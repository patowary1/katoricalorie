Brown Basmati Rice Nutrition

Official KatoriCalorie Recipe & Nutrient Profile

Brown Basmati Rice is a whole-grain alternative to standard white Basmati rice. It retains the nutrient-dense outer bran and germ layers, which supply rich dietary fiber, magnesium, phosphorus, and essential B vitamins.

By keeping the bran intact, brown basmati digests slower in the intestinal tract, resulting in a moderate Glycemic Index (GI). This makes it an ideal staple grain for diabetics, active fitness trackers, and weight management programs.
