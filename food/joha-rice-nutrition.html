Joha Rice (Aromatic) Nutrition

Official KatoriCalorie Recipe & Nutrient Profile

Joha Rice (জোহা চাউল) is a premium, indigenous short-grain aromatic rice variety grown in the fertile soils of the Brahmaputra valley. It is internationally recognized for its sweet, distinct aroma, which differs from Basmati rice.

Beyond its scent, Joha is rich in phenolic compounds, antioxidants, and exhibits high protein digestibility compared to commercial polished grains. It makes a highly nutritious staple for daily meals.
