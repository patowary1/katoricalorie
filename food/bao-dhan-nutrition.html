Bao Dhan (Red Rice) Nutrition

Official KatoriCalorie Recipe & Nutrient Profile

Bao Dhan (বাও ধান) is a unique deepwater red rice variety grown traditionally in the floodplains of the Brahmaputra River. It thrives in deep waters and requires zero chemical inputs, making it naturally organic.

Its deep red color is due to high concentrations of anthocyanin antioxidants. Bao Dhan is globally recognized as a superfood due to its exceptional iron, zinc, and dietary fiber content. It supports steady glucose metabolism and increases blood oxygenation.
