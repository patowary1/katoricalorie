Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to KatoriCalorie.in. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any information shared through our platform. This Privacy Policy details how we handle data across our regional nutrition network.

1. Data Collection & Analytics

KatoriCalorie is a privacy-first platform. We do not require user accounts, email registration, or passwords to use our food and activity calculators. To analyze traffic trends and optimize your experience, we utilize Umami Analytics—a non-invasive, privacy-focused tracking architecture. Umami does not collect cookies, personal identifiable information (PII), or track users across external sites.

Users may opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or via www.aboutads.info.
