Our Story & Creator's Vision

Welcome to KatoriCalorie. We are a platform created out of a simple but vital realization: that fitness and metabolic health should never require you to abandon the foods of your childhood, your family, or your culture.

The Gap in Modern Fitness Apps

If you have ever tried using a standard calorie tracker, you have probably noticed a massive gap. These platforms are built for Western diets—focusing heavily on salads, protein shakes, and oats. When they do feature Indian foods, they are limited to mainstream national dishes like Paneer Tikka, Chicken Biryani, or butter-laden Dals.

For those of us raised on traditional Assamese curries, Naga smoked meats, and regional boiled greens, modern logging tools are useless. Trying to log a simple bowl of sour fish curry (Masor Tenga), green papaya starter (Omita Khar), or indigenous wild herbs (Dhekia Sak) leads to frustration. You are forced to guess, swap your meals for foods you don't enjoy, or give up tracking altogether.

Bridging Tradition and Science

KatoriCalorie is here to bridge that gap. We specialize in mapping the nutritional profiles of **Northeast and Assamese regional delicacies** alongside mainstream Pan-Indian staples.

We do this through strict database synthesis. Every single food item in our catalog has its macronutrient values carefully calculated by cross-referencing reliable dietary databases, including the ICMR-National Institute of Nutrition (NIN) Hyderabad, the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI), and the USDA.

Our Dual Focus

  • Preserving Cultural Identity: We believe that traditional cooking methods—especially the low-oil, steam-based, and fermented cooking styles of Northeast India—are inherently healthy. By indexing them, we ensure these ancestral recipes remain relevant in a modern, health-conscious society.
  • Providing EEAT-Compliant Metrics: Our calculator does not use random guess metrics. We transparently compute your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) using the scientifically validated Mifflin-St Jeor Equation, helping you adjust your portions based on mathematical energy balances.

We hope KatoriCalorie helps you reach your wellness goals while enjoying every single spoonful of your traditional home-cooked meals. Health is not about restriction; it is about portion awareness and metabolic balance.
