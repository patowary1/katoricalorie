About KatoriCalorie

The Vision Behind India's Regional Nutrition Platform

Welcome to KatoriCalorie.in, a platform dedicated to demystifying the nutritional profiles of traditional Indian, Assamese, and Northeastern cuisines. Most mainstream fitness applications assess food metrics using Western metrics (grams, ounces, or cups), which fundamentally clash with how meals are served and consumed in regional Indian households.

Our Core Mission: To bridge the gap between regional culinary heritage and modern health tracking by utilizing the traditional, universally understood unit of volume: the Katori (bowl).

Why We Built This

Whether it is a daily comforting bowl of Masor Tenga, the unique alkaline profile of Khar, or staples like rice and dal, estimating portion sizes accurately has always been an uphill battle for people trying to manage their weight in regional communities. KatoriCalorie was engineered from the ground up to empower users to track their traditional meals effortlessly without complicating their relationship with food.

The Creator's Vision

Developed by an independent software innovator and developer from Assam, KatoriCalorie is built out of a profound passion for regional food sustainability and digital public utility tools. Driven by the philosophy that health tools should accommodate cultural identity—not replace it—this platform serves as India's regional food nutrition knowledge gateway. We combine technical clean interfaces with authoritative data points to keep tracking accessible, lightning-fast, and completely private.

Empowering healthier regional lifestyles, one katori at a time.
