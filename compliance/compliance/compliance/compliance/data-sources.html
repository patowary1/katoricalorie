Data Sources & Nutrition Methodology

Transparency in Caloric Computations & Medical Frameworks

1. Authoritative Institutional References

To ensure maximum computational credibility under strict digital guidelines, our baseline macronutrient parameters are closely mapped against validated biochemical databases, including:

  • ICMR - NIN (Indian Council of Medical Research - National Institute of Nutrition, Hyderabad): Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT) for regional specific ingredients and raw grain macro matrices.
  • FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India): Nutritional indexing guidelines and serving evaluation metrics.
  • USDA (United States Department of Agriculture) National Nutrient Database: For standard global whole foods, basic proteins, and raw ingredients comparison data.

2. Traditional "Katori" Standardized Assumptions

Since standard home recipes diverge, KatoriCalorie models its volumetric calibrations using standard culinary hardware measurements:

  • 1 Standard Katori (Medium Bowl): Equivalent to approximately 150ml to 180ml of fluid/gravy volume capacity.
  • Dry Staples (Rice/Poha): Calibrated based on a lightly packed katori volume, correlating to roughly 80g–100g of cooked weight baseline.
  • Exercise & Activity Burn: Formulated using uniform MET (Metabolic Equivalent of Task) values combined with standard individual weights to estimate expenditure.

3. Review Process & Maintenance

Our nutritional architecture undergoes continuous auditing against published public health litany updates. Data structural tables are verified iteratively to maximize computation precision for regional households.
