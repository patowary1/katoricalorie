Medical Disclaimer & Awareness

IMPORTANT: The calculations, food values, and information provided on this platform are intended strictly for educational, informational, and general meal-planning purposes.

Not Medical or Clinical Advice

KatoriCalorie is not a medical organization, clinical institution, or healthcare provider. The content found on this website, including but not limited to the Mifflin-St Jeor BMR calculator, active plate logs, calorie burner estimates, and nutritional blogs, does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or clinical treatment plans.

We do not offer medical opinions or prescribe diet plans for specific clinical conditions. Our services are designed as tools to assist healthy individuals in gaining general awareness of their calorie and portion sizes.

Caloric and Metabolic Estimations

The formulas utilized by KatoriCalorie (such as the Mifflin-St Jeor equation) are mathematical models based on statistical averages. While these equations are highly validated in nutrition science, they remain estimates.

Your actual Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) can vary significantly based on genetics, body composition (muscle mass vs. fat mass), thyroid function, hormonal profiles, and underlying metabolic conditions.

Nutrient Value Variations

The calorie and macronutrient values of foods listed in our database (especially regional Assamese and Northeast delicacies) are subject to variation. Factors such as local ingredient variations, cooking temperatures, exact ingredient weights, and seasonal source differences can change the final caloric density of a dish.

Consult a Healthcare Professional

You should consult with a qualified physician, registered dietitian, or certified health practitioner before:

  • Starting any caloric deficit or weight loss program.
  • Making major changes to your dietary structure (e.g., transition to high-protein, low-carb, or strict alkaline diets).
  • Beginning an intensive physical exercise routine or activity track.

Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking treatment because of something you have read or calculated on KatoriCalorie.
